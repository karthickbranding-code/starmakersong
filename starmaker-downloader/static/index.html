Recording Downloader

Save your own StarMaker recordings as audio or video files.

How to use

  1. Open your recording in the StarMaker app and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Paste the link above, tick the box, and press Get file.
  3. Choose audio or video and the download starts.
Only download recordings you own or have permission to save. Backing tracks and other people's performances are protected by copyright and StarMaker's terms. This tool is not affiliated with StarMaker.
